One sentence, in the voice of an alien tourist, giving planet Earth a one-star review while slipping in a real compliment about the food. The locals pay for water that falls from the sky and worship glowing rectangles — yet the tacos alone nearly earned this planet a second star.

## How it works

`main.py` assembles the sentence from three fragments (rating, complaint, turn) and validates it before writing: exactly one sentence, 1–200 characters, no trailing newline. The checked line goes to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
